Roof inspection

Roof inspection

We inspect Perth roofs for leaks, damaged coverings, ridge and flashing defects and drainage problems. Our team explains the findings and provides clear repair and maintenance priorities.

Request a roof assessment

Roof condition, access and repair priorities

An inspection can investigate an active leak, assess roof condition or plan maintenance. The agreed purpose determines the accessible coverings, ridges, valleys, flashings, penetrations and drainage to examine. Earlier findings and repair history are useful when a leak returns or proposals differ.

Roof inspection and repair recommendations

  1. Agree the assessment purpose, accessible areas and any requested photographs or written records before booking.
  2. Inspect the relevant roof and drainage components, relate findings to the reported concern and explain limitations or further investigation.
  3. Set out repair priorities and a separate written repair quote, distinguishing urgent defects from planned upkeep.

Roof inspections are chargeable: confirm the fee and scope before booking. Access to concealed or unsafe areas is excluded.

Inspection components, fees and records

Roof inspection components, fees and records

The agreed inspection checks safely accessible roof coverings, ridge caps, valleys, flashing, gutters and downpipes against your concern. Ellis confirms the accessible areas, written findings and any inspection fee before booking.

Repair quote defects, methods and included work

A repair quote should identify the defect, roof area, components, materials, access and work included. Ellis explains any concealed areas requiring further investigation and obtains agreement before additional work.

Pre-winter roof defect and drainage inspection

Arrange a check of damaged tiles, loose capping, flashings, valleys and debris in gutters before seasonal rain. Ellis plans safe access and prioritises the maintenance and repairs found; keep your own observations at ground level.
