PRACTICAL ENQUIRY NOTES

PREPARE A ROOF INSPECTION ENQUIRY.

  • State why the roof is being checked and the area involved.
  • Share safe exterior or interior observations and when they were noticed.
  • Provide the Perth suburb, roof material where known and relevant access context.

SAFE PREPARATION

RECORD, DO NOT RISK ACCESS.

Use ground-level observations and photographs where safe. Avoid climbing onto a roof, entering an unsafe ceiling space or disturbing a roof component to gather evidence.
