SERVICE QUESTIONS

ROOF INSPECTION & MAINTENANCE PERTH — PRACTICAL QUESTIONS.

What should a roof inspection include?

The agreed inspection checks safely accessible roof coverings, ridge caps, valleys, flashing, gutters and downpipes against your concern. Ellis confirms the accessible areas, written findings and any inspection fee before booking.

What should a roof repair quote identify?

A repair quote should identify the defect, roof area, components, materials, access and work included. Ellis explains any concealed areas requiring further investigation and obtains agreement before additional work.

How should a property be prepared for Perth winter rain?

Arrange a check of damaged tiles, loose capping, flashings, valleys and debris in gutters before seasonal rain. Ellis plans safe access and prioritises the maintenance and repairs found; keep your own observations at ground level.
