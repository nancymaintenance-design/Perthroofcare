CITY BEACH / REPAIR PRIORITIES

Ridge lines, upper junctions and gutters

Exposed ridge and hip lines

For a City Beach roof, we inspect ridge caps, hip connections and adjoining tile or sheet edges for movement and damaged fixings. Tile ridge repairs and metal capping repairs use methods suited to their different materials.

Upper roof junctions

We check penetrations and changes in roof plane together with the uphill sheet or tile laps. A recurring rain leak is traced through these connected details before we repair the affected flashing or covering.

Water reaching the eaves

The roof edge, gutter run and downpipe outlet are checked as one drainage route. We identify damaged supports, leaking seams and restricted discharge and include the relevant repair or clearing work.

We confirm the repair scope after inspecting the property and explain the affected components, compatible materials and access arrangements. Your suburb, roof type, leak timing and existing photographs help our team prepare for the visit.

Roof-edge symptoms and uphill water paths

Describe the affected gutter run, outlet and nearby roof junction. Water at the eave can need a connected roof and drainage assessment rather than a gutter patch alone.
