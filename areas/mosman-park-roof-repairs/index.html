MOSMAN PARK / REPAIR PRIORITIES

What we check and repair.

Separate each roof elevation

At a Mosman Park property, we identify which roof face and building level is affected before tracing the connected ridge, valley and gutter. This keeps the work focused when different roof sections drain in different directions.

Roof-to-wall transitions

Where an extension or lower roof meets a wall, we inspect flashing laps, fixings and the adjoining covering. We repair the transition as a connected detail so the work addresses the junction and the water arriving above it.

Coastal and sheltered details

We inspect exposed metal edges as well as sheltered areas where deposits can remain. The repair scope distinguishes local fixing or flashing deterioration from damage to the surrounding roof covering.

We confirm the repair scope after inspecting the property and explain the affected components, compatible materials and access arrangements. Your suburb, roof type, leak timing and existing photographs help our team prepare for the visit.
