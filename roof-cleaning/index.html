Roof cleaning

Roof cleaning

We clean tile and metal roofs in Perth, checking roofing, old coatings and drainage before agreeing the method. Painting is a separate service.

Cleaning dirt and debris from the roof

Cleaning removes dirt, deposits and debris. It does not repair broken tiles, rusted metal or leaking joints. We check the roofing and any existing paint before choosing a method. If spray painting is also agreed, we clean the roof first.

Choosing the cleaning method and areas

  1. Inspect the roof material, deposits, loose parts and previous coatings before choosing the cleaning method.
  2. Agree the areas to be cleaned, safe access and protection of nearby surfaces. List any repairs or valley, gutter and outlet clearing separately.
  3. Check the agreed areas and runoff after cleaning. Further preparation may be needed before a coating can be applied.

Cleaning, painting and inspection are different services

Booking cleaning without painting

Cleaning can be arranged on its own. We inspect the roof condition and discuss what you want to achieve before agreeing whether cleaning alone is suitable.

Agreeing an inspection separately

A cleaning job is not automatically a roof inspection. We agree the cleaning and any separate assessment before arranging the work, so you know what is included.
