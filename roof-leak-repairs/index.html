Roof leak repairs

Ceiling stains and leaks during rain

A ceiling stain can be some distance from where rain enters the roof. Tell us whether the leak appears in steady rain, wind-driven rain or after rain has stopped. You do not need to wait for another downpour to contact us. Dampness in dry weather may come from plumbing, condensation or equipment and may need another trade.

Finding the leak before choosing a repair

  1. Inspect the reported leak and nearby roof areas, including previous sealant patches or repairs. A visible stain or fresh patch does not establish the source.
  2. Agree the repairs, materials, safe access and written quote before repairing the damaged tile, sheet, fixing, flashing or drainage connection.
  3. Check the repaired connections and water path. Explain any areas we could not inspect and whether further investigation is needed.

If the leak started after solar panels were installed, tell us the installation date and when you first noticed water. Timing alone does not establish the cause. Electrical equipment must be handled by the appropriate specialist.

Rain conditions and previous leak repairs

Leaks in heavy rain or wind-driven rain

Heavy rain puts more water through valleys and gutters. Wind can drive rain towards roof laps and flashings. We check these areas and the timing of the leak before recommending repairs.

Recurring leaks around valleys and flashings

Worn joints, damaged flashing and faults around roof openings can let water in. We inspect these areas with the nearby tiles or sheets and drainage, rather than adding another patch without checking the cause.

Roof leak findings and the repair quote

We compare indoor water marks with the roof, flashings, valleys and drainage above and nearby. We explain what we found, the repairs proposed and any access limits or further checks in the written quote.
