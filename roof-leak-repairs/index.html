Roof leak repairs

Roof leak repairs

We trace roof leaks to their source and repair damaged tiles, metal sheets, fasteners, valleys and flashings. From ceiling stains to recurring rainwater entry, our Perth team checks the connected water path and completes the required roof repairs.

Roof inspections are chargeable. Confirm the fee and scope before booking.

Rain patterns and signs of roof leaks

A ceiling stain marks where moisture becomes visible, which can be away from the entry point. We relate heavy or wind-driven rain, dry-weather dampness and recurring leaks to tiles or sheets, ridges, valleys, flashings and drainage. Plumbing, condensation or equipment faults may require another trade.

Investigating recurring roof leaks

  1. Trace the reported water path and review previous sealing or repairs; a visible stain or fresh patch does not establish the source.
  2. Repair the identified tile, fixing, sheet, flashing or drainage defect with materials suited to the adjoining roof. Agree the access, scope and written quote first.
  3. Review the repaired connections and water route, explaining access limits or further investigation appropriate to the leak.

For a leak noticed after solar installation, share the installation and leak dates. Timing alone does not establish the cause. Electrical equipment must be handled by the appropriate specialist.

Valleys, flashings and penetration checks

Heavy and wind-driven rain leak investigation

Heavy or wind-driven rain can expose a failed overlap, flashing junction or drainage restriction that is less apparent in lighter rain. Ellis traces the entry path on site and checks the reported weather pattern before specifying the repair.

Recurring leaks at valleys, flashings and penetrations

Yes. Flashing, valleys and penetrations can admit water when their connections or adjoining materials deteriorate. Ellis inspects these junctions and the drainage route to identify the cause of a recurring leak.

Roof leak assessment and connected components

The assessment connects the indoor stain or drip with roof coverings, flashings, valleys, penetrations and drainage. Ellis explains the findings, repair plan and written quote, including any access limits or further investigation.
