PROFESSIONAL SERVICE SCOPE

ROOF LEAK REPAIRS PERTH — TRACE THE WATER-ENTRY CONTEXT.

Roof leak repairs and leak investigation start with the point where water is seen, but that point may not be the entry location. Rain can move along roof members, sarking or connected surfaces before a ceiling mark or drip becomes visible.

A useful Perth roof leak repair enquiry connects indoor evidence with the roof detail above or nearby: a tiled-roof valley, chimney flashing, skylight or penetration, ridge, gutter edge, metal sheet lap or corroded fixing. This helps separate a visible symptom from an assumed repair method.

PRACTICAL ENQUIRY NOTES

PREPARE A ROOF LEAK REPAIR ENQUIRY.

  • Record the room and location where water or staining is visible.
  • Note the rain or wind conditions linked to the observation.
  • Use safe photographs and avoid entering an unsafe roof space.

SAFE PREPARATION

RECORD, DO NOT RISK ACCESS.

Use ground-level observations and photographs where safe. Avoid climbing onto a roof, entering an unsafe ceiling space or disturbing a roof component to gather evidence.
