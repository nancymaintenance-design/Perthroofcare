Metal roof repairs

Metal roof repairs

We repair Perth metal roofs, including corrugated sheets, fasteners, washers, ridge and hip capping, flashings and penetrations. Our team checks corrosion, damaged connections and water paths before completing the appropriate local repairs.

Roof inspections are chargeable. Confirm the fee and scope before booking.

Service content

Fixings, sheets and formed junctions

A metal roof fixing relies on the screw, washer and surrounding sheet together. Replacing a screw may not address corrosion around its hole. We assess corrugated iron, tin and COLORBOND descriptions against the actual profile and finish, then distinguish local fastener or flashing work from affected-sheet replacement.

Our solution

  1. Inspect corrosion, loose sheets, fixings, laps, ridge and hip capping, roof edges and penetrations with the connected water path.
  2. Agree compatible fasteners, sheet or formed-flashing details and the extent of removal. Local repair depends on serviceable surrounding material.
  3. Review seating, repaired laps and adjoining connections after the agreed work, with exclusions and any wider replacement requirement explained.

Solar panels and roof equipment can affect access and the investigation area. Electrical or equipment work requires the appropriate specialist; roofing photographs do not establish a fault in that equipment.

Scope details

Metal fastener and sealing washer defects

Yes. Loose or deteriorated screws and washers can allow water through a fixing point. Ellis checks the fasteners, adjoining sheets and overlaps to confirm whether fixing repairs or additional work are required.

Ridge, hip and flashing connections on metal roofs

Capping or flashing needs attention where it is loose, damaged or no longer sheds water at a ridge, hip or junction. Ellis checks the adjoining sheets and connections, then quotes for the affected roof sections.

Local metal repairs where adjoining sheets remain sound

A local repair is possible when the surrounding sheets and connections remain serviceable. Ellis inspects corrosion, fixings, laps and flashing to decide whether targeted repairs or replacement sections suit the roof.
