Roof repairs for commercial properties

Commercial roof repairs can include sheets, capping, flashings, roof openings, gutters and downpipes. We plan the work with the building contact, allowing for operating hours, occupied areas, roof equipment and access restrictions. The authorised party must confirm who can approve the work.

Planning access and work around the site

  1. Review affected rooms, leak timing and previous repairs, then inspect the roof and drainage areas that can be reached safely.
  2. Agree materials, safe access, protection of occupied areas and the work sequence with the authorised contact before starting.
  3. Check the repairs and provide the photos or written records agreed for the job. Discuss any extra work separately.

If you need photos or written records, tell us before booking so we can confirm what is available. Equipment and electrical work must be handled by the appropriate specialist.

Site information and work approval

Operating hours and safe roof access

Tell us how the building is used, when it is occupied and any roof access restrictions. Details of leaks, previous repairs and roof equipment help us plan the inspection with the building manager.

Sheeting, flashings and drainage repairs

We inspect damaged sheets, flashings, roof-opening connections, gutters and downpipes together where the faults are related. The quote identifies the repairs and the agreed work sequence.

Information from the building contact

Provide the affected room or area, leak timing, previous repair records and the authorised site contact. Existing photos or a plan can help, but are optional. Tell us about access and operating requirements.
