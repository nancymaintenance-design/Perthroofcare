Commercial roof repairs

Commercial roof repairs

We repair roofs for Perth businesses and managed properties, covering sheets, flashings, penetrations and drainage. Our team coordinates inspection, site access and repair work with the building manager.

Roof inspections are chargeable. Confirm the fee and scope before booking.

Service content

Roof repairs around building operations

Commercial work can involve sheets, capping, flashings, penetrations, gutters and downpipes. We coordinate the assessment and repair scope with the building contact, considering operating hours, occupied areas, roof equipment and restrictions on access. Shared components do not establish who can approve the work.

Our solution

  1. Connect affected rooms and leak timing with accessible roof and drainage details, reviewing previous repair records.
  2. Agree materials, access, protection of occupied areas and the work sequence with the authorised contact before repairs begin.
  3. Review the repaired components and provide the findings or records agreed for that scope; arrange any additional work separately.

Confirm required photographs or written records before booking. A repair assessment is not automatically an engineering report, certification or an insurance determination. Equipment and electrical work need the appropriate specialist.

Scope details

Commercial roof materials, operations and site access

The building use, roof material, affected areas, access arrangements and operating hours help us plan commercial repairs. We also review recurring leaks, previous work and equipment or penetrations on the roof, then coordinate the repair scope with the building manager.

Commercial flashing, penetration and drainage repairs

Yes. Our commercial service covers connected sheet, flashing, penetration, gutter and downpipe defects. We inspect the roof and drainage route, identify the affected components and organise the repair sequence around access and the property operation.

Building manager records and authorised site contact

Provide an affected-area plan or room locations, photographs, leak timing, previous repair records and a site contact. Tell us about restricted access and operating requirements. We use these details to plan inspection, explain the work and arrange practical attendance.
