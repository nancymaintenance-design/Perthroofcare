SCOPE / 15

Read the condition before choosing a path.

Roof surfaces, edges, drainage lines and junctions can each give a different piece of information. This page is a practical reading guide, not a diagnosis. Note what is visible, where it appears and whether it changes after weather or routine use.

Ellis Services Group presents Perth roof repair context in plain language so a conversation can begin with useful detail. Materials, access and the relationship between roof and drainage all shape which questions matter next.

OBSERVE / COMPARE

Keep the observation specific.

  • Look for changes at joins, edges, penetrations and water paths.
  • Separate a visible surface mark from the wider roof context around it.
  • Record material type when known, without assuming a cause.
  • Use the same reference point when comparing conditions over time.
