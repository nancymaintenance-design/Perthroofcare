Strata roof defects, authority and access

The lot or building, affected room and roof level help define an enquiry. The authorised contact must confirm permission to inspect shared or adjoining areas and identify who approves work. Roof or drainage arrangements alone do not determine ownership, payment liability or common-property boundaries.

Strata roof assessment and repair scope

  1. Review leak history, occupied areas and access restrictions; agree requested photographs or written records before booking.
  2. Define the affected roof or drainage components, materials and approval arrangements, including how additional work or access to another lot is agreed.
  3. Review the agreed work and records with the authorised contact, noting any exclusions or further assessment.
