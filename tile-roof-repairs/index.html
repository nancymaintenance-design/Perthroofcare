PROFESSIONAL SERVICE SCOPE

Replacing damaged tiles and restoring adjoining roof details

We repair concrete and terracotta tile roofs across Perth. Cracked, broken, displaced and missing tiles are checked with the surrounding covering so replacement pieces fit the roof profile and overlapping arrangement. Our scope identifies the tiles and local roof areas needing work.

Where tile damage connects with loose ridge caps, a valley or chimney flashing, our team coordinates the adjoining repairs. We check tile seating and the finished interfaces within the agreed scope. This restores the damaged roof detail rather than covering it with a cosmetic finish.

Cracked tiles and compatible replacements

Cracked tiles and connected water entry

A cracked, broken or missing tile may allow rain past the covering, depending on the position and surrounding roof detail. It should be assessed with the overlaps and adjoining components. A crack is relevant evidence, but it does not by itself explain every ceiling mark or establish that the whole roof needs restoration.

Individual tile replacement and compatible profiles

Local replacement can suit a few damaged concrete or terracotta tiles where the surrounding roof remains serviceable. An older tile needs a compatible profile and overlap, not merely a similar colour. Spare tiles and supplier availability can help establish a match; availability is checked rather than guaranteed in advance.

SERVICE GUIDANCE

Tile profiles, ridges and flashing repairs

Compatible concrete and terracotta replacement tiles

Yes. Individual concrete or terracotta tiles can be replaced where a suitable profile is available and the surrounding roof supports a local repair. Ellis checks alignment, adjoining damage and material availability; new tiles can differ in colour from weathered tiles.

Tile repairs connected to ridges, valleys and chimney flashing

Include ridge, valley or chimney flashing work when inspection finds damage at those connections or a linked water-entry path. Ellis identifies the affected components and includes the required repairs in the written quote.

Tile damage location, roof material and access information

Share your suburb, the approximate damaged tile area and any indoor leak or recent storm change. You do not need to identify the cause. Photos are optional and can be emailed; do not climb onto the roof to obtain them.
