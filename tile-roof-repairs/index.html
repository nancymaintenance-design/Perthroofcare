Tile roof repairs

Tile roof repairs

We repair concrete and terracotta tile roofs across Perth, replacing damaged tiles and restoring adjoining ridge, valley and flashing details. Our inspection identifies the repair area and compatible materials for the roof.

Request a roof assessment

Cracked, slipped or missing roof tiles

We replace damaged concrete and terracotta tiles where the surrounding covering supports a local repair. Matching means a compatible profile, fit and overlap, not colour alone; spare tiles and supplier availability are checked before a match is confirmed. Damage beside a ridge, valley or chimney can require connected repairs.

Matching tiles and repairing adjoining junctions

  1. Inspect cracked, displaced or missing tiles, the supporting detail and adjacent ridge, valley and flashing connections.
  2. Source compatible tiles, agree the affected area and access, then replace damaged components and restore alignment and adjoining details as required.
  3. Review tile overlaps and the repaired junctions. Explain any unmatched material, wider deterioration or excluded work in the written scope.

Two-storey height, roof pitch and structures below the work affect controlled access. Do not climb onto the roof to check a tile or obtain photographs.

Tile replacement, roof access and hidden damage

Compatible concrete and terracotta replacement tiles

We can replace individual concrete or terracotta tiles where a suitable profile is available and the surrounding roof supports a local repair. We check alignment, adjoining damage and material availability; new tiles can differ in colour from weathered tiles.

Tile repairs connected to ridges, valleys and chimney flashing

When our inspection finds damage or a linked water-entry path at a ridge, valley or chimney flashing, we identify the affected components and include the required repairs in the written quote.

Tile damage location, roof material and access information

Share your suburb, the approximate damaged tile area and any indoor leak or recent storm change. You do not need to identify the cause. Photos are optional and can be emailed; do not climb onto the roof to obtain them.
