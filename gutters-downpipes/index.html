Gutters & downpipes

Gutters & downpipes

We clean and repair gutters and downpipes across Perth. We check the gutter, outlet and pipe together when water overflows or leaks at a connection.

Gutters that overflow after cleaning

A full gutter and a leaking downpipe may be part of the same problem. We inspect the gutter, outlet, pipe joints and brackets together. Leaves are not the only cause of overflow. If the gutter still overflows after cleaning, tell us where it happens and whether water also leaks from the downpipe. Underground drains are not automatically included in roof drainage work.

Checking the gutter and downpipe together

  1. Inspect where water overflows or leaks and follow the visible route through the gutter, outlet and downpipe.
  2. Agree any clearing, joint repairs, replacement gutter or pipe sections and bracket repairs, using suitable materials.
  3. Check the repaired connections and explain any drainage restrictions or downstream work that is not included.

Building height, restricted access and nearby roof damage affect the work. A description from ground level is enough to start an enquiry; photos are optional.

Blockages, joints and water backing up

Causes of overflow beyond leaves and debris

Poor fall, a blocked outlet, damaged joints or a restricted downpipe can also cause overflow. We inspect the visible drainage route and explain the clearing or repairs needed.

Checking outlets and connected downpipes

When water backs up or escapes near an outlet, we inspect the gutter and pipe together. This helps identify the restriction instead of treating the gutter and downpipe as separate faults.

Optional photos taken from the ground

You can email existing or ground-level photos of the gutter, outlet, downpipe or water marks. A written description is enough to request an assessment. Avoid ladders or roof access to collect images.
