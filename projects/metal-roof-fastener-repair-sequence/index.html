The metal roof before the fastener work

The first photograph establishes the overall roof condition before the recorded work. Close views then show corroded fastener points and a ridge-area detail. This progression matters because the fixings form part of a larger sheeted roof: their condition needs to be understood alongside the nearby covering and roof junctions, rather than viewed as an isolated spot.

Our recorded fastener repair work

The work-stage photograph shows fastener work taking place on the metal roof. It connects the earlier condition photographs with the final overall view of the completed surface. The sequence therefore presents the affected details, the recorded work and the finished roof together. It is a practical example of our targeted metal roof repair work around fixing points.

Why the surrounding roof remains part of the story

Fasteners secure roof components, while the sheet profile, nearby overlaps and ridge details shape their surroundings. The close photographs make the corroded fixing areas visible; the overall photographs explain where those details sit. Bringing these views together helps property owners understand the difference between a small visible component and the connected roof area that informs its repair scope.

From site detail to the completed surface

The final photograph shows the roof surface after the recorded fastener work. Viewed alongside the installation image and earlier condition details, it completes the visual account of this job.
