TILE ROOF CHIMNEY FLASHING REPAIR PERTH.

OUR PROJECT / ELLIS SERVICES GROUP

Project story updated 2026-10-09

A chimney at the meeting point of tiled slopes

The brick chimney sits beside a valley between tiled roof planes. That layout brings two important water-management details together: the metal around the chimney base and the channel carrying water through the valley. The wide project photograph establishes their position in the roof, while the close views show how the formed metal meets the adjacent tiles.

The chimney and valley work we completed

The completed photographs show grey formed metal around the chimney, including the apron at its base and the stepped side detail against the brickwork. They also show the adjoining metal valley extending between the tiled slopes. Our work connects these details within the existing roof layout, with the photographs presenting the completed junction from several useful angles.

An apron and side flashing with different roles

The apron is the formed metal projecting from the chimney base over the adjoining roof surface. The side flashing follows the chimney-to-roof junction along the sloping tiles. These are connected parts of the same detail, with the valley close by. The project explains why chimney work needs careful attention to both the masonry interface and the neighbouring roof covering.

The finished chimney junction in its roof setting

The wider photographs show the completed flashing alongside the valley, tiles and nearby ridge lines. The close photographs show the apron and stepped metal detail. This combination makes the completed work easy to understand and demonstrates our focus on connected roof junctions. Our flashing repairs service handles chimney details, valleys and the adjoining tiled or metal roof connections.
