DOCUMENTED PROJECT / ROYLEYSTONE, WA

METAL ROOF FASTENER LEAK REPAIR IN ROYLEYSTONE.

A documented site-detail case study about ageing, rusted metal-roof fastener points and a customer-approved targeted repair discussion.

Make an enquiry

OBSERVED CONDITION

Fastener points were part of the water-entry context.

At this Roleystone, WA property, ageing and rust around metal-roof fastener and rivet points were identified as part of the water-entry context. Visible corrosion at a fixing point can be useful information, but roof materials, access and the wider water path still need to be considered together.

The customer explained that the available budget was limited. The discussion therefore focused on an economical scope that addressed the visible fastener details without presenting a broader roof treatment as the only option.

REPAIR APPROACH

A customer-approved targeted scope for this property.

1 / Prepare the affected detail

The agreed work focused on the affected metal-roof fastener areas and the condition visible around them.

2 / Structural adhesive

For this property, structural adhesive was used around affected fastener areas as part of the agreed repair approach.

3 / Renewed coating

Coating was renewed by spraying as part of the customer-approved targeted scope discussed with the customer.

Roof materials, corrosion, access and the wider water path differ from property to property, so a similar approach is not assumed to suit every roof.
