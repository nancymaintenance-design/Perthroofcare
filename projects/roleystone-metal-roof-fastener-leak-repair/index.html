OUR PROJECT / ELLIS SERVICES GROUP

Project story updated 2026-10-09

Ageing fastener areas on a Roleystone metal roof

The site record identifies ageing and rust around metal roof fastener and rivet points as part of the water-entry problem at this property. The close photographs show corrosion, aged coating and existing sealant around the affected details. These views established the condition being addressed and supported a focused discussion about the repair work and the customer’s priorities.

A targeted scope agreed with the customer

The customer explained that the available budget was limited. We developed an economical, targeted scope around the affected fastener areas instead of presenting broader roof treatment as the only option. The agreed work followed the recorded site condition and the customer’s requirements. This approach made the included tasks clear and kept the repair focused on the areas identified for attention.

Structural adhesive and renewed spray coating

For this property, our recorded repair used structural adhesive around affected fastener areas. We also renewed the coating by spraying as part of the customer-approved scope. These are the documented materials and work items for this job. The close photographs show the roof details that informed the repair, including the condition around fixing points and adjoining sheet surfaces.

A defined repair with a clear service connection

The completed scope combined local fastener-area treatment with coating renewal at this Roleystone property. It demonstrates our ability to organise targeted metal roof work around the site condition and the customer’s priorities. Our metal roof repairs service addresses fixing areas and connected roof details, while roof leak repairs provides the route for investigating and repairing water-entry problems.
