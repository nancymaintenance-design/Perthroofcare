Box gutter repairs

Box gutter repairs

We repair box gutters in Perth for leaks, overflow and deteriorated connections. We assess the internal gutter with the roof and outlets before agreeing the repair scope.

Request a roof assessment

Box gutter channels, joints and overflow

A box gutter collects water within connected roof details. Its channel, joints, outlet and overflow route need assessment together. Indoor staining alone cannot distinguish a leaking joint, retained water or overflow. Clearing debris and repairing deteriorated gutter material are different jobs.

Box gutter clearing and repair options

  1. Relate rain conditions and earlier clearing to the channel, adjoining junctions and outlet condition.
  2. Define local joint work, affected lengths or drainage changes, checking compatibility with retained materials rather than assuming a coating suits every gutter.
  3. Review the repaired connections and explain remaining drainage limitations and any further work needed.

Include building height, affected level and authorised access contact. The photographs record original conditions and work in progress.

Perth box gutter repair scope

Box gutter restrictions and defects beyond clearing

Clearing debris does not repair damaged materials or resolve every outlet or drainage restriction. For recurring overflow, we assess the connected system to identify the work required.
