PERTH ROOF CARE / OUR COMPANY

Ellis Services Group

Roof repairs and connected drainage, with a scope shaped around your property.

Perth Roof Care is operated by Ellis Services Group Pty Ltd. Our Perth-based team serves local properties from our office at 140 St Georges Terrace, Perth WA 6000.

We inspect, repair and maintain roofs and their connected drainage across Perth. Our work covers roof leaks, damaged tiles, metal sheets and fixings, ridge capping, flashing, gutters and downpipes.

Roof inspections are chargeable. Confirm the fee and scope before booking.

OUR REPAIR SERVICES

Roof repairs and drainage, from the surface to the outlet.

Leaks, tiles and metal roofs

We investigate water entry and inspect the surrounding roof before specifying repairs. Localised work can address damaged tiles, metal sheets or fixings where the assessed condition allows.

  • Roof leak repairs
  • Tile roof repairs
  • Metal roof repairs

Ridges, valleys and flashing

We assess ridge mortar, valley condition and flashing at roof junctions. The findings determine whether the work needs local repair, repointing, rebedding or replacement of affected components.

  • Ridge capping & repointing
  • Roof valleys & flashing repairs
  • Roof repairs

Drainage, inspections and maintenance

We check gutters, outlets and downpipes as a connected route, then quote for clearing or repairs. Roof inspections and maintenance help identify deterioration and plan the work needed.

  • Gutters & downpipes
  • Roof inspection
  • Roof maintenance

OUR WORKING APPROACH

Assess the roof. Explain the work.

Our team assesses the property, explains the findings and provides a repair plan and written quote before work begins. The repair scope follows the condition of your roof.

The quote identifies the affected components, materials and access needed for the agreed work. Local repairs, wider roof work and optional surface treatments are considered according to the assessed condition.

Share your suburb, roof type if known and the issue you have noticed. Existing ground-level photographs are optional; there is no need to access the roof to make an enquiry.
