WHAT WE HELP YOU DISCUSS

Roofline details, materials and water paths.

A roof is made up of connected elements rather than isolated parts. Tiles or metal sheets meet ridges, valleys, flashings, penetrations, gutters and downpipes. A visible change in one place can be easier to explain when the nearby materials and the direction of water flow are also noted. The purpose of the website is to make those starting points easier to recognise and describe in plain language.

For a roof repair enquiry, it can help to state whether the observation is on a tile roof or metal roof, whether it is near a ridge, valley, flashing or penetration, and whether the change appears on the outside, inside or both. For a drainage enquiry, it can help to identify the gutter section, outlet, downpipe or ground connection involved. A simple description is enough: the aim is not to diagnose the issue before getting in touch.

QUESTIONS PEOPLE OFTEN ASK

Questions people often ask before contacting us.

What should I include in a roof repair enquiry?

Start with the property location, the visible detail you noticed, where it appears and when you first saw it. A brief note about nearby roof materials, drainage components or recent weather can add helpful context. You do not need to know the cause before making contact.

Can I ask about gutters or downpipes as well as the roof?

Yes. Gutters, outlets and downpipes form part of the visible drainage route from the roof edge. Describe the section that concerns you and what you observe during or after rain where known.
