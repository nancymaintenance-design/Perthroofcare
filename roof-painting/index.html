Roof Painting Perth

Roof spray painting in Perth, with cleaning first and a finishing scope suited to the inspected roof. Required repairs and preparation are agreed before the finish is applied.

Arrange an assessment

Start with material and existing coating

Tell us whether the roof is tile or metal, if known, and whether it has been painted previously. The existing material and finish affect preparation and product suitability. We confirm the proposed system for the actual roof rather than promising one product for every surface.

Cleaning, preparation and spray painting

Our sequence is cleaning followed by spray painting. The quote should distinguish necessary defect repairs, cleaning, preparation and finishing so that the work can be compared clearly. Surface work should not conceal an unresolved leak.

What the painting quote should explain

Confirm the areas included, proposed products, access arrangements, protection of adjoining property and completion checks. Product details, coating life and applicable warranty terms belong in the agreed scope; no universal duration is promised here.

Questions before booking

Do you clean the roof before spray painting?

Yes. Cleaning comes before spray painting. The existing roof and proposed finish also determine the necessary repairs and preparation.

Which paint system will be used?

The proposed product system needs to be confirmed for your roof material and existing finish in the quote. No single product is assumed suitable for every roof.

Discuss your property and the next step

Send your suburb, roof material if known, affected area and previous work details. Inspections are chargeable; confirm the fee and required records before booking. Existing safe photographs are optional. We are open Monday–Sunday, 9am–9pm Perth local time.

Understand the roof problem before choosing the work

Does roof painting fix roof leaks?

Painting is a surface-finishing stage, not a substitute for identifying water entry. Our sequence is cleaning before spray painting, with required defect repairs and surface suitability established as part of the agreed scope. Confirm the preparation and product system for the actual roof; a fresh appearance does not prove a leak has been corrected.
