Existing roof coatings and surface condition

Roof painting is a finishing decision for a suitable covering. Existing coating, weathering and damage affect product compatibility and preparation; colour alone does not determine the system. Leaks and component repairs remain distinct from appearance work.

Roof cleaning and painting preparation

  1. Assess the actual roof material, existing coating and defects before agreeing the surface and product scope.
  2. We clean the roof before spray painting. The sequence separates necessary repairs, cleaning and preparation from the chosen finish.
  3. Review the agreed finished areas and exclusions. Product details and any applicable written terms must be confirmed for the property.

The photographs show the roof after spray painting and do not show spray application in progress. They cannot establish coating life, leak correction or suitability for another roof.
