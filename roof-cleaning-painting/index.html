PROFESSIONAL SERVICE SCOPE

Roof cleaning and painting — repair, preparation and finish

Cleaning removes deposits from suitable roof surfaces and drainage areas; painting involves a prepared substrate and a compatible coating system. We inspect the existing tile or metal finish, identify local defects and specify the cleaning work separately from coating preparation.

Broken tiles, deteriorated fixings and damaged flashings need repair before finishing. Our team explains the preparation, surface work and adjoining drainage protection for the job. Coating product selection and application requirements follow the actual substrate and the agreed scope, not a single package for every roof.

PREPARE A ROOF CLEANING OR PAINTING ENQUIRY.

  • Describe the roof material and the surface change or finish you want us to assess.

Repair defects before deciding on a surface finish.

Does roof painting fix a leaking roof?

Painting is a surface treatment, not a substitute for repairing cracked tiles, damaged sheets, loose connections or defective flashing. Repair needs and coating suitability should be established before preparation or finishing is agreed.

Ask the quote to distinguish repairs, cleaning, preparation and the proposed finish. Existing material and coating condition affect what can be used. If your priority is a leak rather than appearance, start with a leak assessment and decide on any broader surface work separately.

SERVICE QUESTIONS

Questions about roof cleaning & painting perth.

What should be assessed before roof cleaning or painting work begins?

We inspect the roof material, existing coating, surface condition, damaged components and drainage before specifying cleaning or painting. Repairs come first; the cleaning method, preparation and coating system must suit the actual substrate and existing finish.

Can roof cleaning and painting be discussed for both tile and metal roofs?

Yes. Tile and metal roofs need different preparation and coating considerations. We check tile condition and porosity or the metal roof finish and corrosion, address repair defects and select compatible cleaning and coating methods for the surface.

What details should be included in a roof cleaning and painting enquiry?

Tell us whether the roof is tile or metal, its existing finish, any previous coating and whether there are leaks or damaged components. Photographs help show the roof area and surface condition. We inspect the property and provide a scope covering repairs, preparation and the proposed finish.
