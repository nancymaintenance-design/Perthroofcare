Roof restoration

Roof restoration

We restore Perth roofs through coordinated tile or metal repairs, ridge and flashing work, drainage maintenance and suitable surface preparation. Our inspection establishes the work your roof needs before cleaning or coating is specified.

Roof inspections are chargeable. Confirm the fee and scope before booking.

Request a roof assessment

Service content

Coordinated repairs and surface work

Restoration brings suitable component repairs together with agreed cleaning, preparation and finishing. A few broken tiles or an isolated leak may need only a local repair. Wider work is assessed against the covering, material condition and your objective; painting is not a substitute for a sound roof detail.

Our solution

  1. Assess tiles or sheets, ridge support, flashings and drainage to establish whether a broader programme is appropriate.
  2. Separate repair items from cleaning, preparation and finish in the quote, identifying materials, deterioration addressed and preparation required. We clean before spray painting where painting is agreed.
  3. Review the agreed repair and finishing scope, drainage details and exclusions. Confirm additional work, product information and any warranty terms in writing.

Access, removal of damaged material and disposal should be clear in the comparison. Whole-roof replacement is a separate decision where retained material cannot support an appropriate repair.

Scope details

Identified leak repairs within restoration scope

Leak-related defects are identified during inspection and included in the repair scope before surface finishing. Our team checks tiles or sheets, ridges, flashings and drainage and explains the required work.

Metal roof restoration and material suitability

A metal roof can suit coordinated repairs and surface work where its condition supports that approach. We inspect corrosion, sheet integrity, fixings and existing finish and specify local replacement or broader work accordingly.

Restoration assessment, repairs and finishing scope

We inspect the covering and connected roofline, record damaged components and assess the existing surface. The scope sets out repairs, access, preparation, proposed finish and drainage work so each stage is clear.
