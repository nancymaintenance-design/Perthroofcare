SCOPE OF WORK

One coordinated plan for your roof.

We restore Perth roofs through coordinated tile or metal repairs, ridge and flashing work, drainage maintenance and suitable surface preparation. Our inspection establishes the work your roof needs before cleaning or coating is specified.

Repair before finishing

We identify broken tiles, unstable ridge caps, deteriorated metal fixings and damaged flashing first. These defects need repair before surface work; a new coating alone does not repair a broken component.

Preparation matched to the roof

Tile and metal surfaces need different preparation. We check existing coatings, corrosion and substrate condition, plan appropriate cleaning and specify compatible materials for the proposed finish.

Drainage and completion

Valley channels, gutters and outlets are checked alongside the roof surface. The completed scope brings together the repair details, surface work and drainage maintenance appropriate to the property.

Contact Ellis to arrange an on-site assessment. We explain the findings, agree the repair plan and provide a written quote, including the proposed materials, deterioration addressed and preparation required. Existing photographs are optional.

Roof restoration quote inclusions

Roof restoration is a coordinated scope of suitable repairs and any agreed preparation or finish. Its cost depends on the roof condition and the work included, not just its area or appearance. A quote for cleaning and painting is not directly comparable with a quote that also repairs tiles, ridge support, flashings or drainage.

Establish whether wider work is appropriate

We inspect the defects before recommending restoration. A roof with one affected component may need a local repair. If the surrounding material is extensively deteriorated, a surface finish alone does not establish that restoration is suitable. Ask what the assessment found, what can be retained and which work is needed before preparation starts.

Restoration repairs, cleaning, preparation and finishing

  • List the damaged components and affected runs included in the repair work.
  • Identify cleaning and preparation appropriate to the existing material and finish.
  • Confirm the proposed finishing system, where it is to be applied and the relevant product requirements.
  • Include access arrangements, protection of adjoining areas, disposal and checks of the completed work.

Height, pitch, access, existing coatings and the extent of component repairs can all affect the scope. Clarify exclusions, optional items and how any newly exposed defects will be discussed before additional work proceeds. We do not publish a single restoration price for every Perth property.

Gutter replacement as an identified restoration item

Not automatically. Gutter repairs, replacement of an affected section and wider drainage work need to be identified in the agreed quote. Confirm any whole-system replacement separately; it is not implied by the word restoration.

Structural assessment outside surface restoration

No. Restoration and roof repair observations are not automatically an engineering or whole-building assessment. If there are wider structural concerns, discuss the appropriate assessment before choosing repairs or a surface finish.

Compare local repairs and wider roof work, then review the separate roof cleaning scope and roof painting scope. Discuss an assessment for your roof.

Restoration repairs, preparation and finishing

A local repair addresses identified defects in a defined area. Restoration coordinates a wider set of suitable repairs and any agreed preparation or finishing. The inspected condition determines whether that wider scope adds value; appearance alone does not identify a leak repair.

Our restoration quote identifies component repairs, drainage work, access, preparation and finishing. Optional surface work is distinguished from the repairs addressing defects. A single affected component can suit targeted repair before broader work is considered.

Roof restoration and component repairs — service guidance

Identified leak repairs within restoration scope

Leak-related defects are identified during inspection and included in the repair scope before surface finishing. Our team checks tiles or sheets, ridges, flashings and drainage and explains the required work.

Metal roof restoration and material suitability

A metal roof can suit coordinated repairs and surface work where its condition supports that approach. We inspect corrosion, sheet integrity, fixings and existing finish and specify local replacement or broader work accordingly.

Restoration assessment, repairs and finishing scope

We inspect the covering and connected roofline, record damaged components and assess the existing surface. The scope sets out repairs, access, preparation, proposed finish and drainage work so each stage is clear.
