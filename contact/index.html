What happens next?

Include your suburb, roof material if known, where the problem appears and when you noticed it. Existing photographs are helpful; there is no need to access the roof.

Our team reviews the details with you, arranges the next step and explains the proposed repair scope before work begins.

Before you send your roof repair enquiry.

Tell us whether the issue is an active leak, damaged component or planned maintenance. Include any previous repair history and the contact authorised to arrange access. Ask about inspection charges, the records you need and any access restrictions before a booking is confirmed.
