What a Roof Inspection Checks Before Perth Winter Rain

A roof inspection identifies the covering and drainage defects that need attention before repairs or maintenance are arranged. Ellis Services Group inspects Perth tiled and metal roofs, explains the findings and organises the work appropriate to the property. Inspection identifies the problem; maintenance and repair address it.

Inspecting tiles and metal sheets before wet weather

We look for cracked, missing or displaced tiles and examine adjoining ridge and junction details. On metal roofs, inspection includes sheet condition, exposed fixings, laps and formed capping relevant to the property.

Roof age alone does not define the repair scope. The actual material condition and the extent of local damage determine whether work involves isolated replacement, connection repairs or a broader component scope.

Checking ridge, wall and penetration connections

Ridge and hip capping, roof-to-wall flashings and penetrations join different roof surfaces or materials. We inspect their stability and connections alongside the reported leak area or maintenance concern.

Chimneys, vents and other openings need attention to the adjoining covering as well as the flashing itself. The findings should identify the affected components and the work needed, rather than listing a vague roofline concern.

Following drainage through valleys, gutters and downpipes

Leaves and deposits can restrict channels and outlets, while corroded material or disconnected joints require repairs. We inspect the water route and distinguish clearing tasks from damaged components.

Our valley and gutter cleaning project illustrates maintenance linked with local tile work. It provides a useful example of related work items; it does not establish a fixed maintenance interval or a universal checklist for every building.

Turning inspection findings into repair priorities

We explain the defects found, affected areas and recommended sequence of work. The proposed scope identifies materials, access and included tasks so customers can distinguish urgent repairs from maintenance that can be planned.

Before the visit, provide the suburb, roof material if known, leak history and existing photographs. Keep roof access to our team. After inspection, ask which items need repair, how those repairs connect with drainage and what completed-work records are included.

Related questions

Is inspection the same service as ongoing roof maintenance?

Inspection identifies roof condition and priorities. Maintenance includes the agreed clearing, upkeep and local repairs that follow those findings; the scope distinguishes each task.

What will the proposed roof repair scope explain?

It identifies affected components, recommended work, materials, access and included tasks. Our team explains repair priorities and separates them from future maintenance items.

Keep observations at ground level.

Do not climb ladders, access roofs or approach damaged electrical equipment to collect information. Do not work from a roof or elevated gutter without controlled access. Photographs are optional and do not replace an on-site assessment by Ellis Services Group.

Before arranging the work

Is the inspection fee separate from the repair quote?

Confirm the inspection fee and what it includes with our team before you book. Also ask whether any inspection charge is separate from the repair work or credited against an accepted quote. This website does not publish a universal inspection fee or assume that every inspection is free.

Explain whether you need photographs, written repair recommendations or a particular report format. Confirm what can be supplied for your property before booking. A roof condition inspection is not automatically a pre-purchase building inspection, structural assessment or insurance report. Access limitations should be recorded alongside the findings.
