Roof Maintenance Perth — Tiles, Metal Roofing, Valleys & Drainage

Prepared by Ellis Services Group · Updated 2026-10-10

Roof maintenance in Perth combines inspection of the covering with practical work on the parts that carry rainwater away. Ellis Services Group checks tiles, metal sheets, ridges, flashings, valleys, gutters and downpipes, and organises maintenance and repairs according to the condition of the property.

Tile roof maintenance and ridge condition

Cracked or displaced tiles, weathered ridge pointing and debris in valleys need inspection before rain exposes a weakness. We check the adjoining tile field and ridge bedding as well as the visible defect. Repointing maintains the joint finish; unstable bedding requires the appropriate rebedding work.

Individual tile replacement is planned around the existing profile and available matching material. A maintenance inspection helps identify local damage early and separates routine cleaning from repair work. We explain the work required for the affected tiles, ridge runs and roof junctions.

Metal roof maintenance: fixings, laps and flashings

Metal roof maintenance includes inspection of exposed screws, sealing washers, sheet overlaps and formed capping. We also check corrosion and debris around roof edges and penetrations. The remaining sheet condition determines whether a local fixing repair is appropriate or whether the damaged metal needs replacement.

Repair materials need to suit the existing roof. Our team checks compatible connections and records local defects before the work is arranged. Cleaning or coating is not a replacement for repairing damaged fixings, sheets or flashings; those components are dealt with in the repair scope.

Valley, gutter and downpipe maintenance

Leaves, sediment and other debris can interrupt the drainage route between the roof and its outlets. We inspect valleys, gutter runs and connected downpipes, checking both obstructions and the condition of joints and supports. This identifies cleaning needs alongside leaking or deteriorated components.

Maintenance should suit the property surroundings and the roof condition. Nearby vegetation, sheltered roof areas and previous drainage problems help guide the inspection priorities. We explain which tasks are routine maintenance and which require a specific repair or component replacement.

Plan roof maintenance before Perth winter rain

Arrange the inspection before the roof has to handle repeated rain events, particularly if the property has a known leak or drainage history. Provide the suburb, roof type, previous work and safe existing photographs so our team can prepare for the visit.

The maintenance scope records the components needing attention and the proposed work. It can coordinate tile or metal repairs with valley and gutter maintenance, instead of leaving connected tasks to separate enquiries. Contact Ellis Services Group for a roof inspection and practical maintenance plan.

Questions about Roof Maintenance Perth

What information is useful for a roof maintenance enquiry?

Share your suburb, roof material if known, recurring concerns and any previous work. Ellis inspects roof coverings, ridge details, junctions and drainage to set maintenance priorities and provide a written quote.

How can a property be prepared before seasonal rain?

Have damaged roof components, blocked valleys and restricted gutters or downpipes checked before seasonal rain. Ellis arranges safe access and quotes for clearing and repairs; do not climb onto the roof for these checks.

When should a maintenance question connect to a roof repair discussion?

New leaks, displaced components, deteriorated joints or recurring overflow need repair assessment alongside routine maintenance. Ellis identifies the cause and explains which repairs belong in the agreed maintenance scope.

Our team inspects the property and carries out the work described in the agreed repair scope. Explore the matching service and our photographed projects, or contact Ellis Services Group to arrange a visit.

Keep observations at ground level.

Do not climb ladders, access roofs or approach damaged electrical equipment to collect information. Do not work from a roof or elevated gutter without controlled access. Photographs are optional and do not replace an on-site assessment by Ellis Services Group.

Before arranging the work

What should I arrange before Perth winter rain?

Start with known defects and the connected drainage route: damaged tiles or sheets, ridge connections, flashings, valleys, gutters and outlets. Inspection helps separate clearing and upkeep from component repairs. The right interval depends on the roof and its surroundings, not one schedule for every property.

Share the last inspection or repair date, recurring leaf deposits and previous rain-related problems. Existing ground-level photographs can help explain the enquiry. Do not access the roof to gather evidence. Maintenance does not guarantee that a roof will remain leak-free in every future weather event.
