Metal Roofing Perth — Corrugated Roof Materials, Leaks & Repairs

Prepared by Ellis Services Group · Updated 2026-10-14

Metal roof repairs in Perth involve more than the visible sheet surface. Corrugated roofing, roof screws, sealing washers, sheet laps and formed flashings work together to keep rain outside. Ellis Services Group inspects these connected details and carries out targeted repairs to the components that need attention.

Corrugated, tin and COLORBOND roof repair materials

People often describe a metal roof as a tin roof, corrugated iron roof or COLORBOND roof. The profile, coating and condition of the existing sheets matter when planning repairs. We check the sheet shape, overlaps and surrounding finish so replacement materials and fixings suit the roof rather than treating every metal surface as interchangeable.

Local roof sheet replacement addresses a damaged section where adjoining roofing remains suitable. Widespread deterioration requires a broader scope. Our inspection distinguishes surface staining from corrosion that has weakened the sheet, and records the affected sheets, flashing connections and access requirements before the work is arranged.

Roof screws, washers and sheet laps: common leak points

An exposed roof screw relies on a suitable fixing and sealing washer. Weathered washers, loose fixings and corrosion around a fixing hole need inspection alongside the sheet beneath them. Replacing a screw alone is not enough when the surrounding metal is damaged. We check the fastening point and specify the repair for both the fixing and the sheet condition.

Sheet laps and formed capping also need an effective water-shedding arrangement. We inspect overlaps, displaced edges and previous sealant patches together with the direction of rainwater flow. This connects a visible problem with the actual detail that needs repair and avoids repeating a surface patch that leaves an adjoining defect untreated.

Metal roof rust, coastal exposure and maintenance

Corrosion inspection includes roof edges, sheltered areas, fixings and places where debris holds moisture. Properties exposed to salt or nearby vegetation need maintenance suited to those conditions. We assess the affected material rather than assuming that every coastal roof has the same damage or that every rust mark requires whole-roof replacement.

Maintenance combines removal of debris from suitable roof drainage areas with inspection of sheet connections, flashings and gutters. Materials used for repair must be compatible with the existing roofing. Our team explains which areas need local repair, component replacement or planned maintenance, and connects the work to any reported leak history.

Our targeted metal roof leak repair plan

Tell us the suburb, approximate roof age if known, where water appears and whether it follows heavy rain or wind. Existing photographs and previous repair details help us prepare. We inspect the relevant roof covering, fixings, laps, penetrations and drainage route to identify a practical repair scope for the property.

The scope identifies the affected components, proposed materials and access arrangements. Targeted metal roof repairs can include fixings, local sheet replacement and formed flashing or capping work. A coating is a separate surface treatment; the underlying leak-related defects are repaired first. Contact Ellis Services Group to arrange the inspection and repair work.

Fasteners, capping and local sheet repairs

Metal fastener and sealing washer defects

Yes. Loose or deteriorated screws and washers can allow water through a fixing point. Ellis checks the fasteners, adjoining sheets and overlaps to confirm whether fixing repairs or additional work are required.

Ridge, hip and flashing connections on metal roofs

Capping or flashing needs attention where it is loose, damaged or no longer sheds water at a ridge, hip or junction. Ellis checks the adjoining sheets and connections, then quotes for the affected roof sections.

Local metal repairs where adjoining sheets remain sound

A local repair is possible when the surrounding sheets and connections remain serviceable. Ellis inspects corrosion, fixings, laps and flashing to decide whether targeted repairs or replacement sections suit the roof.

Safe ground-level observations before inspection

Do not climb ladders, access roofs or approach damaged electrical equipment to collect information. Do not work from a roof or elevated gutter without controlled access. Photographs are optional and do not replace an on-site assessment by Ellis Services Group.

Sheet condition and fixing compatibility

Local fastener and flashing work on sound sheets

A local fixing or flashing repair may be suitable when the surrounding sheet remains sound. We assess the washer, fixing hole, sheet condition and connected laps together. Where corrosion or damage extends beyond the fixing, the affected sheet or formed component may need replacement.

Corrugated, tin and COLORBOND are terms customers use to describe metal roofs. The actual profile, coating and material compatibility need checking before repair materials are selected. Mention rattling sheets, visible rust and previous screw work. A material name does not imply manufacturer authorisation or a universal repair method.
