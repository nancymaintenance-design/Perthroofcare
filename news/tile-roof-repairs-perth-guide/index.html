Broken Roof Tiles in Perth: Replacement, Matching and Leak Repairs

We repair concrete and terracotta tile roofs in Perth, checking cracked, displaced or missing tiles alongside the ridges, valleys and flashings. We match the replacement tile profile and restore the overlaps and adjoining connections included in the repair scope.

Cracked tiles, slipped tiles and exposed gaps

Cracked tile faces and edges, displaced tiles and missing pieces create different repair requirements. We inspect the affected area and nearby tiles to identify the damage and determine which pieces need replacement or repositioning.

Water entry can also involve a valley or flashing beside otherwise serviceable tiles. We check those interfaces when a broken tile is found near a reported leak, so the repair addresses the connected problem rather than stopping at the most visible damage.

Cracked tiles and connected water entry

A cracked, broken or missing tile may allow rain past the covering, depending on the position and surrounding roof detail. It should be assessed with the overlaps and adjoining components. A crack is relevant evidence, but it does not by itself explain every ceiling mark or establish that the whole roof needs restoration.

Tile roof repairs

Matching concrete and terracotta tile profiles

Tile shape, overlap and fit matter more than a colour-only match. A replacement needs to sit correctly with adjoining tiles and suit the existing roof detail. Our team checks the material and profile before selecting compatible replacements.

Older roofs can require attention to the available tile profile and surrounding condition. We explain the replacement arrangement as part of the scope, including any related tile edges or junctions that need work. We do not treat every concrete or terracotta tile as interchangeable.

Individual tile replacement and profiles

Local replacement can suit a few damaged concrete or terracotta tiles where the surrounding roof remains serviceable. An older tile needs a compatible profile and overlap, not merely a similar colour. Spare tiles and supplier availability can help establish a match; availability is checked rather than guaranteed in advance.

Tile roof repairs

Checking ridge caps and tile-to-flashing connections

Field tiles sit beside bedding, ridge caps and formed metal details. Loose capping or a damaged flashing can affect the same area as a cracked tile. We inspect those connections before setting the full repair extent.

At a chimney or valley, the tile edge and flashing form a water-shedding connection. Our valley and chimney project shows these components together. Such photographs illustrate the relevant roof details; the condition of your own roof determines which parts we repair.

Replacing damaged tiles and reviewing the finished area

The agreed work identifies replacement tiles, adjoining components, safe access and any ridge or flashing repairs. We replace or reposition the damaged tiles and repair the adjoining roof details included in the agreed scope; a surface coating does not replace those repairs.

After the repair, we review tile seating, overlaps and the adjoining roof detail included in the job. Customers can ask for the scope and completed-work record so the distinction between tile replacement, ridge work and flashing work is clear.

Broken tiles and connected leak sources

Broken tiles and other possible water-entry defects

A damaged tile is a repair item, but water can also enter at adjoining ridges, valleys or flashings. We inspect those connections when tracing the leak.

Tile inspection with controlled roof access

Leave roof-level access and tile handling to our team. Ground-level photographs and the location of indoor water marks are useful preparation without disturbing the covering.

Safe ground-level observations before inspection

Do not climb ladders, access roofs or approach damaged electrical equipment to collect information. Do not work from a roof or elevated gutter without controlled access. Photographs are optional and do not replace an on-site assessment by Ellis Services Group.

Tile matching and two-storey access

Individual concrete and terracotta tile replacement

Individual replacement can suit local damage where the surrounding tiled roof remains serviceable. The replacement needs a compatible profile and fit; colour alone is not enough. Older tile availability needs checking before a matching replacement can be confirmed.

Tell us whether tiles are cracked, missing or displaced, and whether water has appeared inside. Do not climb onto the roof to identify a profile. For a two-storey home, mention the roof level, driveway and side access so suitable access arrangements can be assessed before work is booked.
