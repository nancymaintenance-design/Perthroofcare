Repair quote scope and cost comparison

Roof repair costs depend on the assessed scope. Use the questions below to compare quotes and distinguish component repairs from optional surface work. The guide explains cost factors rather than a list of prices.

Inspection fees come before the repair decision

Roof inspections are chargeable. Confirm the fee, the investigation area and any records required before booking. An inspection and a repair quote are different parts of the decision; confirm their separate scope with the team.

Material, components and extent of damage

A broken tile, a leaking metal fixing and a deteriorated valley are different jobs. A compatible replacement tile may need sourcing; a corroded screw hole may require work beyond a new washer. Ask which components are being repaired, replaced or retained and whether adjoining defects are part of the same scope. The ceiling stain alone cannot establish the extent of work.

Access changes the work needed

Height, pitch, narrow side access and structures beneath the roof affect how work can be carried out. We undertake two-storey roof repairs and assess the access arrangements for the actual property. Tell us the roof level and restrictions before the visit; no standard access package or equipment charge is assumed. Weather and safe access can also affect arrangements.

Comparable roof leak repair quote inclusions

A useful quote identifies the findings, affected area, repair items, materials, preparation, access, waste removal and exclusions. Ask how additional deterioration will be recorded and approved. If two quotes propose different components, the totals are not a like-for-like comparison. Request clarification of why the proposed work addresses the identified defect, especially after an earlier repair has failed.

Repair, repointing, restoration or replacement

Local repairs address defined defects. A ridge quote should distinguish repointing from work to the supporting bed. Restoration combines suitable repairs with agreed surface work; cleaning and spray painting are not substitutes for tracing a leak. Whole-roof replacement is a separate scope when broader condition and repair limitations support it. Compare retained material, removal, connections and finishing rather than choosing from appearance alone.

Written terms and the information to send

The written terms for the proposed work establish any warranty scope, duration, exclusions and reporting process. Our team confirms the terms for the particular scope; this guide does not establish a standard warranty. Send your suburb, roof material if known, affected room, timing and previous repair history. Existing photographs are optional; do not climb onto the roof to obtain them. For a rental or strata property, include the authorised contact and access arrangements.

Safe ground-level observations before inspection

Do not climb ladders, access roofs or approach damaged electrical equipment to collect information. Do not work from a roof or elevated gutter without controlled access. Photographs are optional and do not replace an on-site assessment by Ellis Services Group.

Roof repair cost factors and quote inclusions — inspection and work scope

Local repairs where surrounding roofing remains serviceable

Yes. A defined tile, metal, ridge or flashing defect can be assessed for a local repair. We check the surrounding material before setting the scope; a small visible defect can connect with other damage. Cleaning or painting is a separate decision, not a condition of every repair.

For a rental property, include the tenant or access contact and who can approve the work. Ask for the repair items, access, materials, removal of waste and exclusions to be clear in the quote. Any warranty terms should be confirmed in writing for that scope, rather than assumed from a general website statement.
