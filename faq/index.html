Roof Repairs Perth — Frequently Asked Questions

Answers from Ellis Services Group about leak repairs, roof materials, drainage, inspections, quotes and arranging the work.

Leaks and urgent repairs

How do you find the source of a roof leak?

We connect the indoor stain or drip with the roof covering, junctions and drainage above it, then repair the defects identified in the investigation. ROOF LEAK REPAIRS PERTH →

Can you investigate a leak that only happens in heavy rain?

We inspect valleys, outlets, roof laps and flashings and use the rain history to focus the investigation. Repairs follow the actual defects found. ROOF LEAK REPAIRS PERTH →

Can a ceiling mark help you prepare for inspection?

The room location and timing of water entry help us relate the symptom to the roof above. Existing photographs are useful; keep roof access to our team. ROOF LEAK REPAIRS PERTH →

What happens when I report urgent storm damage?

Urgent enquiries are prioritised; attendance is arranged promptly when weather, site access and safety conditions allow. We identify the protection and permanent repairs needed. STORM DAMAGE ROOF REPAIRS PERTH →

Can you check a roof that has already been patched?

We inspect the previous work and adjoining roof details to identify remaining defects and specify the repairs required for the current condition. ROOF LEAK REPAIRS PERTH →

Tiles, metal roofing and roof junctions

Which roof materials do you repair?

Our services cover concrete and terracotta tiles and metal roof sheets, with connected ridge, valley and flashing work. The existing profile and condition guide the materials used. ROOF REPAIRS PERTH →

How do you repair broken or displaced tiles?

We inspect the damaged tiles and adjoining roof, select compatible replacements or repositioning work and restore the affected covering. TILE ROOF REPAIRS PERTH →

Can leaking screws or washers be repaired on a metal roof?

We inspect the fixing, washer and surrounding sheet and carry out compatible fastener work or local sheet repair where needed. METAL ROOF REPAIRS PERTH →

What is the difference between repointing and rebedding?

Ridge capping covers the junction between roof planes. Repointing renews the outer pointing around caps; rebedding restores the supporting bedding. We check cap stability to specify the appropriate work. RIDGE CAPPING REPAIRS PERTH →

What does roof flashing do?

Flashing directs water away from roof-to-wall junctions, chimneys and penetrations. We repair damaged connections and replace affected formed metal where required. ROOF VALLEYS & FLASHING REPAIRS PERTH →

Can you repair valleys and chimney flashings together?

We inspect the connected junction and coordinate repairs to the valley, chimney flashing and adjoining tiles or sheets where the defects are related. ROOF VALLEYS & FLASHING REPAIRS PERTH →

Why do roof edges need checking during a repair?

Roof edges connect the covering with flashings, fascia and drainage. We check adjoining damage and drainage connections when they affect the repair area. ROOF REPAIRS PERTH →

Gutters, downpipes and maintenance

Does gutter overflow always mean the gutter needs replacing?

We distinguish debris or an outlet restriction from damaged joints, support problems and corrosion. The scope identifies cleaning, adjustment or replacement work. GUTTER REPAIRS PERTH →

Do you repair downpipe joints and brackets?

We repair affected joints, supports, pipe sections and gutter outlet connections along the visible drainage route. DOWNPIPE REPAIRS PERTH →

Can gutters and downpipes be handled as one job?

We inspect the connected water route and coordinate the required clearing and component repairs in one scope where appropriate. GUTTERS & DOWNPIPES PERTH →

What does roof maintenance include?

Maintenance includes the agreed condition checks, drainage clearing and local repairs to tiles, fixings, ridges and flashings. We set priorities from the property condition. ROOF MAINTENANCE PERTH →

Can you inspect the roof before winter rain?

We inspect the covering, junctions and drainage, explain identified defects and organise repair and maintenance priorities before wet weather. ROOF INSPECTION PERTH →

Is cleaning the same as painting or restoration?

Cleaning, coating preparation and repairs are different tasks. We specify surface work for the tile or metal material and address damaged components before a finish is applied. ROOF CLEANING & PAINTING PERTH →

Inspection, quotes and arranging the work

What should I send before a roof inspection?

Send the suburb, roof material if known, affected area, leak timing, previous repair details and existing photographs. Our team uses them to prepare the visit. ROOF INSPECTION PERTH →

Do I need to climb onto the roof for photographs?

No. Photographs are optional and do not replace an on-site assessment. Use existing images or photographs from a safe ground-level position only. Our team arranges the roof inspection and access required for the work. ROOF INSPECTION PERTH →

What should a roof repair quote explain?

The scope should identify the affected components, proposed work and materials, access arrangements and included tasks. Ask our team about any item needing clarification. ROOF REPAIRS PERTH →

How do you choose between repair and component replacement?

We assess the extent of damage and condition of surrounding material. Local defects can suit targeted repairs; deteriorated components can require replacement. ROOF REPAIR OPTIONS PERTH →

Do you work on commercial and managed buildings?

We coordinate inspection and repairs with the building manager, including site access, affected tenancies and operating requirements relevant to the work. COMMERCIAL ROOF REPAIRS PERTH →

Can I compare an affected area with a nearby roof section?

Existing photographs of both areas can help explain what changed. We inspect the actual material and connections before specifying the repair. ROOF INSPECTION PERTH →

Fees, equipment and scope questions.

Is the inspection fee separate from the repair quote?

Confirm the inspection fee and what it includes with our team before you book. Also ask whether any inspection charge is separate from the repair work or credited against an accepted quote. This website does not publish a universal inspection fee or assume that every inspection is free. Discuss the related scope →

What if a leak appeared after solar panels were installed?

Tell us when the solar installation and the first leak occurred, and share any earlier repair records. Timing alone does not establish the cause. The roof covering, fixings, penetrations and connected drainage need assessment; electrical equipment must be handled by the appropriate specialist. Discuss the related scope →

Does dampness without rain always need a roof repair?

No. Condensation, plumbing or equipment can also be relevant. Note whether moisture follows rain or occurs in dry weather and explain any nearby equipment. The enquiry should be directed to the appropriate assessment rather than assuming that sealing the roof will resolve it. Discuss the related scope →

Is water around a skylight always a flashing problem?

No. Water can involve the skylight unit, its flashing, adjoining roof covering or another nearby source. The location of the indoor mark does not distinguish them. We assess the roof junction within our repair scope and explain when the unit or equipment needs a separate specialist. Discuss the related scope →

What should I confirm about a repair warranty?

Ask for the terms applicable to the agreed work, including duration, covered components, exclusions and how to report a concern. Keep the accepted scope and repair records. If the area leaks again, provide the timing and previous work details so the current condition can be reviewed; do not assume a website describes the terms of your particular job. Discuss the related scope →

Can I book an internal or box gutter repair from this page?

Describe the system and affected area to the team first. Blockages, outlet condition and damaged material can require different work. Scope, access and any specialist requirements need confirming; the external gutter service description does not establish that every internal drainage job is included. Discuss the related scope →

Need an answer for your particular roof?

An answer here explains the general decision, not a diagnosis from a symptom alone. Include the roof material, suburb, timing of water entry and previous repairs when contacting the team. Leave roof access and close-up investigation to the inspection.
