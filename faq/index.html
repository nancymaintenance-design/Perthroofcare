Leaks, stains and previous repairs

Why does my roof only leak during heavy rain?

Heavy rain can reveal a water-entry or drainage problem not apparent in lighter rain. Note the timing and affected room so the connected roof and drainage can be assessed.

Read the related service explanation

Why does my roof leak when it is windy?

Wind can change how rain reaches roof connections. An inspection needs to relate the weather pattern to the covering, laps and flashings rather than assuming one cause.

Read the related service explanation

Does a ceiling stain after rain identify a roof leak?

A rain-related ceiling stain may indicate water entry, but the mark alone cannot identify the source. Roof water entry, plumbing, equipment or condensation can be relevant. Record when and where it appears so the appropriate roof area or other moisture source can be assessed.

Read the related service explanation

Why can a ceiling stain appear away from the roof defect?

Water may travel along connected materials before it becomes visible on the ceiling. The entry point and stain can differ, so the inspection needs to assess the connected water path rather than blame the roof area directly above the stain.

Read the related service explanation

Why can a roof leak return after repairs or sealing?

A recurring leak can involve an unresolved source, a connected defect or changed conditions. Sealant cannot substitute for identifying the source and assessing adjoining components. Provide previous work records and the new leak timing before another repair or surface patch is specified.

Read the related service explanation

How do you find the source of a roof leak?

We connect the indoor stain or drip with the roof covering, junctions and drainage above it, then repair the defects identified in the investigation. ROOF LEAK REPAIRS PERTH →

What happens when I report urgent storm damage?

Urgent enquiries are prioritised; attendance is arranged promptly when weather, site access and safety conditions allow. We identify the protection and permanent repairs needed. STORM DAMAGE ROOF REPAIRS PERTH →

What if a leak appeared after solar panels were installed?

Tell us when the solar installation and the first leak occurred, and share any earlier repair records. Timing alone does not establish the cause. Tiles and ridge capping

Can damaged roof tiles be repaired or replaced individually?

Cracked, broken or displaced tiles can allow water entry. The tiles and adjoining roof need inspection to establish the connected water-entry path. Local replacement or repositioning can suit damage where the surrounding covering remains serviceable. Compatible tile profiles, fit and availability need checking before the replacement and restored alignment are confirmed.

Read the related service explanation · TILE ROOF REPAIRS PERTH →

What is the difference between roof repointing and rebedding?

Repointing addresses exposed pointing, while rebedding renews the supporting bed. The stability and condition of the ridge determine which work is needed.

Read the related service explanation

How do I know if ridge capping needs repair?

Visible cracks, missing mortar or displaced ridge tiles warrant assessment. The condition of the support and surrounding tiles determines the repair, not the crack alone.

Read the related service explanation

Valleys, flashings and metal roofs

What is a roof valley?

A valley directs water where roof slopes meet. Its channel, adjoining tiles and downstream drainage need to be considered together.

Read the related service explanation

When does valley iron need replacing?

Replacement may be appropriate where deterioration means the valley material cannot support a suitable repair. Cleaning alone does not repair damaged metal.

Read the related service explanation

What does roof flashing do?

Flashing manages water where roof materials meet another surface or opening. The formed detail and adjoining connections work together.

Read the related service explanation

What are the signs of damaged roof flashing?

Lifted or separated edges, corrosion and rain-related stains can indicate a flashing problem. The junction and surrounding roof need inspection to establish the cause.

Read the related service explanation

Can roof screws and washers cause leaks?

Yes. Fixings, washers and the surrounding metal can contribute to water entry. Assess the whole fixing point before deciding whether replacement alone is suitable.

Read the related service explanation

Can a metal roof be repaired without replacing every sheet?

Often a local scope can be considered, depending on the sheet and connected components. Widespread deterioration may call for a broader assessment.

Read the related service explanation

Is a leaking skylight caused by the skylight or the roof around it?

A leak can involve the skylight unit, flashing, adjoining roof covering or another nearby source. The position of an indoor mark does not distinguish them. We assess the roof junction within our repair scope and explain when the unit or equipment needs a separate specialist. Roof repairs do not automatically include skylight replacement.

Drainage and other moisture sources

Does gutter overflow need clearing, repair or replacement?

Overflow can affect adjoining eaves and roof-space details. Debris, restricted outlets, damaged joints, support problems and corrosion call for different work. We assess the gutter, outlet and downpipe together to specify clearing, adjustment, local repairs or replacement; clearing alone may not resolve recurring overflow.

Read the related service explanation · GUTTER REPAIRS PERTH →

What causes box gutter overflow and how do I arrange repairs?

We undertake box gutter repairs. Blockages, outlet restrictions, damaged material or drainage limitations can cause overflow. Describe the system, affected area and overflow history so the box gutter, connected outlets and overflow route can be assessed. The repair scope and access are agreed for the particular property.

Read the related service explanation · Discuss the related scope →

Is it a roof leak, condensation or a plumbing leak?

Timing and location help direct investigation but cannot confirm or exclude a roof leak. Explain whether moisture follows rain or occurs in dry weather, recent rain, previous repairs, nearby plumbing and equipment. Condensation, plumbing and equipment faults may need a separate assessment; sealing the roof is not assumed to resolve them.

Existing photographs and a short timing record help explain the enquiry. Keep observations at ground level and away from damaged electrical equipment. Do not enter a roof space or climb onto the roof to identify the source.

Repair, restoration and replacement

How do repair, cleaning, painting and restoration differ?

Repair targets identified defects. Cleaning and coating preparation are separate surface tasks. Restoration combines suitable repairs with agreed preparation or finishing for the tile or metal material; it is not automatically necessary for a local leak. Damaged components need addressing before a finish is applied.

Read the related service explanation · ROOF CLEANING & PAINTING PERTH →

Does roof painting fix roof leaks?

Painting a roof or covering a ceiling stain does not identify or correct the moisture source. Investigate and repair the relevant defects before the agreed cleaning, preparation and spray-painting stages. A surface finish alone does not establish that a leak has been repaired.

Read the related service explanation · roof leak repair service · water-entry investigation guide

When is roof replacement more suitable than repair?

Replacement may be more suitable when the roof cannot support an appropriate repair. Ask for the findings and a clear comparison of retained and replaced components.

Read the related service explanation

Is a second roof assessment worthwhile?

It can help clarify an unexplained recurring leak or different repair proposals. Agree the assessment scope and fee, and provide earlier records.

Inspection, quotes and winter preparation

What should a roof inspection include?

Confirm the purpose, accessible components, limitations, inspection fee and available records. A roof assessment is not automatically a whole-building, engineering or insurance report.

Read the related service explanation

What should I look for in a roof repair quote?

Check the repair items, materials, access, preparation, waste removal, exclusions and approval of additional work. Confirm inspection charges and applicable written terms separately.

Read the related service explanation

How should I prepare my roof for Perth’s winter rain?

Record previous leaks and arrange relevant roof and drainage checks before winter. Maintenance can include agreed condition checks, drainage clearing and local repairs to tiles, fixings, ridges and flashings. The property condition determines priorities. Keep observations at ground level.

What information should I send when reporting a roof leak?

Include the location, timing, affected area, known roof material and previous repairs. Existing safe photographs are optional; identify the person authorised to arrange access.

What photographs can I safely send before an inspection?

Photographs are optional and do not replace an on-site assessment. Existing photographs of the affected area and a nearby section can help explain what changed. Use existing images or a safe ground-level position only; do not climb onto the roof. Our team arranges inspection and access and checks the actual material and connections before specifying repairs.
