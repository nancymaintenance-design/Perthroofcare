PROFESSIONAL SERVICE SCOPE

ROOF VALLEY & FLASHING REPAIRS PERTH — MANAGING JUNCTIONS IN THE WATER PATH.

Roof valleys and flashings direct water at junctions where tiles, sheets, walls, chimneys, skylights and penetrations meet. A visible rust mark, lifted edge, debris build-up or stain can be relevant, but it needs to be understood with the surrounding water path.

Valley repairs, valley iron replacement and flashing repair discussions consider material condition, connection points and the roof surface feeding into the junction. This avoids treating one visible mark as proof of an identical cause or scope on another property.

PRACTICAL ENQUIRY NOTES

PREPARE A VALLEY OR FLASHING REPAIR ENQUIRY.

  • Identify whether the detail is at a valley, wall junction, chimney, skylight or penetration.
  • Record visible corrosion, debris, staining or edge changes from a safe position.
  • Note the roof material and any rain-related interior observation.

SAFE PREPARATION

RECORD, DO NOT RISK ACCESS.

Use ground-level observations and photographs where safe. Avoid climbing onto a roof, entering an unsafe ceiling space or disturbing a roof component to gather evidence.
