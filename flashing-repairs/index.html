PROFESSIONAL SERVICE SCOPE

Repairing valleys and formed flashings at roof junctions

We repair and replace formed metal details where roof surfaces meet walls, chimneys, skylights and penetrations. Valley channels are inspected with the adjoining tiles or sheets and the drainage outlet. Our scope identifies damaged material, disconnected edges and junctions requiring repair.

The work restores water-shedding connections suited to the existing roof. We assess previous patches, select compatible material and repair adjoining covering details as needed. Connected gutters and outlets are checked where they affect the repaired valley or flashing route.

FLASHING AND VALLEY REPAIR METHOD

FIVE FLASHING AND VALLEY DETAILS WE REPAIR.

  1. Roof-to-wall flashing

    Roof-to-wall junctions rely on flashing and adjoining roof surfaces to direct water away from the building. We inspect the connection, surrounding material and any visible water marks as one repair area; where water entry is present, see Roof Leak Repairs Perth.

  2. Chimney and penetration flashing

    Chimneys, pipes and other penetrations interrupt the roof covering. We assess the flashing profile, transitions and nearby roof material before planning the repair work.

  3. Skylight and opening junctions

    Skylights and roof openings need their surrounding flashing and roof covering considered together. We inspect the visible edges, water path and adjoining surface rather than treating a single sealant line as the whole repair.

  4. Tile valleys and valley linings

    Valleys collect water from adjoining roof slopes and must be considered with the tile condition, valley lining and discharge point. For damaged or displaced tiles beside a valley, see Tile Roof Repairs Perth.

  5. Metal roof flashings and formed capping

    On metal roofs, formed flashings, ridge capping, sheet laps and fasteners work together at a junction. We inspect and repair the connected metal-roof detail; see Metal Roof Repairs Perth.

Separate roof junction defects from equipment faults.

Is water around a skylight always a flashing problem?

No. Water can involve the skylight unit, its flashing, adjoining roof covering or another nearby source. The location of the indoor mark does not distinguish them. We assess the roof junction within our repair scope and explain when the unit or equipment needs a separate specialist.

The same distinction matters around vents, evaporative coolers and roof-to-wall connections. Include the affected opening and when water appears. A valley with leaves may need clearing, while deteriorated valley iron can need component work. We separate debris removal from repair or replacement of damaged metal.

SERVICE QUESTIONS

Questions about roof valleys & flashing repairs perth.

What does roof flashing do?

Flashing directs water away from junctions around walls, chimneys, skylights and roof penetrations. Ellis checks its condition and connections to the adjoining roof, then confirms the junction repair and quote.

When does a roof valley need cleaning, repair or replacement?

Cleaning suits debris in an otherwise sound valley. Corrosion, damaged lining or faulty connections can require repair or replacement. Ellis checks the valley and surrounding roof surfaces before quoting for the work.

Why should a skylight leak be assessed with the surrounding roof detail?

A leak near a skylight can involve its flashing, the adjoining tiles or sheets, or water arriving from higher on the roof. Ellis inspects the connected roof area and traces the entry path before confirming the repair.
