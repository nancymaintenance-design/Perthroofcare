Roof replacement

Roof replacement

Whole-roof replacement and re-roofing in Perth, based on an assessment of the existing roof and a clearly agreed removal and replacement scope.

Request a roof assessment

Whole-roof condition and retained components

We undertake whole-roof replacement in Perth. Widespread deterioration or material unable to support a suitable local repair can justify a wider scope. The assessment compares what can be retained, the covering to remove and the proposed replacement; age or appearance alone is insufficient.

Local sheet replacement or whole-roof replacement

  1. Compare local repair and replacement against the observed defects, adjoining junctions and limitations of retaining the covering.
  2. Define new covering, flashings, penetrations, drainage connections, removal, disposal and access. Agree how concealed deterioration will be recorded and further work approved.
  3. Review the agreed replacement components and completion checks, distinguishing the final scope from construction-stage photographs.

Suspected asbestos requires separate identification and specialist arrangements. The photographs are construction-stage records.
