PRACTICAL ENQUIRY NOTES

PREPARE A ROOF MAINTENANCE ENQUIRY.

  • Record the roofline or drainage detail that has changed over time.
  • Note tree cover, debris and rainfall behaviour from a safe ground-level position.
  • Share the roof material, suburb and any prior repair context where known.

SAFE PREPARATION

RECORD, DO NOT RISK ACCESS.

Use ground-level observations and photographs where safe. Avoid climbing onto a roof, entering an unsafe ceiling space or disturbing a roof component to gather evidence.

SERVICE QUESTIONS

ROOF MAINTENANCE PERTH — PRACTICAL QUESTIONS.

What information is useful for a roof maintenance enquiry?

Share your suburb, roof material if known, recurring concerns and any previous work. Ellis inspects roof coverings, ridge details, junctions and drainage to set maintenance priorities and provide a written quote.

How can a property be prepared before seasonal rain?

Have damaged roof components, blocked valleys and restricted gutters or downpipes checked before seasonal rain. Ellis arranges safe access and quotes for clearing and repairs; do not climb onto the roof for these checks.

When should a maintenance question connect to a roof repair discussion?

New leaks, displaced components, deteriorated joints or recurring overflow need repair assessment alongside routine maintenance. Ellis identifies the cause and explains which repairs belong in the agreed maintenance scope.
