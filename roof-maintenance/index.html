Roof maintenance

Roof maintenance

We maintain Perth tile and metal roofs through inspection, drainage clearing and targeted repairs. Our team prioritises tiles, fasteners, ridges, valleys and flashings according to the condition of your roof.

Request a roof assessment

Roof condition and seasonal maintenance

Planned maintenance combines clearing deposits with checks of tiles or metal sheets, ridges, valleys, flashings and outlets. Broken tiles, loose capping, failed fixings or leaking junctions require defined repair work. Previous leaks, nearby trees and changes since earlier maintenance help establish priorities.

Drainage clearing and roof defect repairs

  1. Inspect the covering and connected drainage against the repair history and accessible condition.
  2. Prioritise debris clearing and necessary component repairs, separating optional cleaning or finishing from defects needing attention.
  3. Review the maintained connections and outlets and identify follow-up items suited to the property.

Observations and photographs should be made from ground level, with roof and elevated gutter access left to controlled work arrangements.

Roof material, repair history and priorities

Roof material, repair history and maintenance priorities

Share your suburb, roof material if known, recurring concerns and any previous work. Ellis inspects roof coverings, ridge details, junctions and drainage to set maintenance priorities and provide a written quote.

Seasonal drainage clearing and roof defect repairs

Have damaged roof components, blocked valleys and restricted gutters or downpipes checked before seasonal rain. Ellis arranges safe access and quotes for clearing and repairs; do not climb onto the roof for these checks.

Repair defects identified during roof maintenance

New leaks, displaced components, deteriorated joints or recurring overflow need repair assessment alongside routine maintenance. Ellis identifies the cause and explains which repairs belong in the agreed maintenance scope.
