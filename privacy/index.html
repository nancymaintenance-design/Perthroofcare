PRIVACY / ENQUIRY INFORMATION

PRIVACY FOR ROOF REPAIR ENQUIRIES.

When you use the contact form, Ellis Services Group receives the name, phone number, email address and enquiry details you choose to provide. This information is used so the enquiry can be reviewed and answered.

Information you choose to send

A roof repair enquiry may include the property suburb, roof material, visible roof or drainage detail, weather timing and photographs you decide to share. Only send information that is relevant to the enquiry and that you are comfortable providing.

How enquiry information is used

Enquiry information is used to respond to the roof repair, roof leak, tile roof, metal roof, ridge, valley, flashing or inspection topic you describe. It is not used by this website to make an automatic property diagnosis.
