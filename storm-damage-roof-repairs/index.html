Roof damage and leaks after a storm

After a storm, displaced tiles, loose capping, damaged sheets and drainage problems need inspection. We prioritise urgent enquiries and arrange attendance promptly when weather, site access and safety allow. Temporary protection and permanent repairs are separate work items. Tell us what is new, what has leaked before and any previous repairs.

Assessment, temporary protection and repairs

  1. From a safe place, tell us when the storm occurred, where water is entering and any displaced roofing you can see.
  2. Inspect safely accessible damage and agree any temporary protection, repairs, materials and access arrangements.
  3. Complete the agreed repairs when conditions allow, check the repaired connections and explain any further investigation needed.

We are open Monday–Sunday, 9am–9pm Perth local time. This is not a 24-hour callout service, and immediate attendance is not guaranteed. Stay clear of electrical hazards, sagging ceilings and fallen materials. Call emergency services for immediate life or electrical danger.

Reporting damage safely

Storm timing and the affected area

From a safe place, note when the storm occurred, the affected room or roof area and any new water entry. Photos are optional; do not enter a dangerous area to take them.

Photos and an on-site inspection

A photo cannot show all hidden damage or the full water path. We inspect the roof on site and explain any areas that need further access before quoting for repairs.

Water, fallen materials and electrical hazards

Tell us about active water entry, sagging ceilings, fallen materials and access restrictions from a safe location. Keep clear of hazards and call emergency services for immediate life or electrical danger. Roof attendance depends on weather and safe access.
