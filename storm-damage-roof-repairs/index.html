PROFESSIONAL SERVICE SCOPE

Urgent roof damage assessment, protection and planned repairs

Storm damage can leave displaced tiles, loose capping, affected metal sheets or damaged drainage connections. Our team establishes the affected areas and access requirements, then arranges the protection and repairs appropriate to the condition found. Report active water entry and the location of damaged roof components when contacting us.

Urgent enquiries are prioritised; attendance is arranged promptly when weather, site access and safety conditions allow. Temporary protection and permanent repairs are distinct work items. We explain the immediate measures and the subsequent covering, flashing or drainage work needed for the property.

SERVICE QUESTIONS

Questions about storm damage roof repairs perth.

What should be recorded after storm damage to a roof?

From a safe place, note the storm timing, the affected room or roof area, water entry and any displaced materials. Contact Ellis to arrange a storm-damage assessment; photos are optional and must not require unsafe access.

Can a storm-related roof issue be assessed from one image alone?

No. One image cannot show concealed damage or the full water path. Ellis assesses the affected roof and connected materials on site and explains any further access needed before quoting for repairs.

What safety information is useful in a storm damage roof repair enquiry?

Tell us about active water, sagging ceilings, fallen materials, electrical hazards and restricted access. Keep clear of dangerous areas. Call emergency services for immediate life or electrical danger; Ellis arranges roof attendance when weather and safe access allow.
