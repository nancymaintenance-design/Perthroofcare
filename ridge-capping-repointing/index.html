We repair ridge capping and carry out repointing and rebedding for Perth tiled roofs. Our team checks cap stability, bedding and adjoining tiles, then restores the affected ridge or hip runs.

PROFESSIONAL SERVICE SCOPE

Repointing, rebedding and repairs to ridge and hip caps

We inspect ridge cap stability, the supporting bedding and outer pointing as separate parts of the repair. Secure caps with deteriorated pointing require different work from loose caps with failed support. Our team explains which ridge or hip runs need repointing, bedding repairs or replacement caps.

The scope also covers damaged neighbouring tiles where they affect the repaired ridge detail. We restore the affected support and finish, then review cap seating and the adjoining covering. This gives customers a clear description of the work rather than using repointing and rebedding as interchangeable labels.

Know which ridge repair your quote includes.

What is the difference between repointing and rebedding?

Pointing is the exposed finish at the ridge cap joints; bedding supports the caps beneath it. Repointing renews the relevant finish. Rebedding addresses the supporting bed where its condition or cap stability requires it. Covering loose support with a new finish does not address the underlying problem.

A ridge quote should identify the affected ridge or hip runs, any damaged caps or adjoining tiles, the bedding and pointing work, and access. Cracks or fallen mortar are useful observations, but inspection determines the work needed. Ask which components are being retained and which are being renewed.

SERVICE QUESTIONS

Questions about ridge capping repairs perth.

What is the difference between repointing and rebedding?

Bedding supports the ridge caps; pointing is the exposed finishing material along their edges. Ellis checks both on site, identifies the sections requiring rebedding or repointing and confirms the repair scope and quote.

What should a ridge capping repair quote include?

The quote should identify the ridge or hip sections, cap stability, bedding or pointing work, adjoining tile repairs, materials and safe access. Ellis records the proposed work and any further investigation in writing.

When should a ridge detail be assessed with surrounding tiles?

Ridge work should include adjoining tiles when they are displaced, damaged or involved in the same leak path. Ellis checks cap position and the surrounding tile overlaps before specifying the ridge repair.
