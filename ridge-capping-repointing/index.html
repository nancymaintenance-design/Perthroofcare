PROFESSIONAL SERVICE SCOPE

RIDGE CAPPING REPAIRS PERTH — REPOINTING, REBEDDING AND ADJOINING TILES.

Ridge capping repairs, repointing and rebedding describe connected but different tile-roof details. The visible mortar condition, cap alignment, bedding beneath the caps and adjacent tiles provide the context for deciding what should be discussed.

A crack or gap along a ridge gives our team a clear starting point for determining the full repair scope. A proper ridge-capping conversation considers whether the change is local or continuous, how the adjoining tiles sit, and whether valleys or water-entry observations are also present.

SERVICE-SPECIFIC ASSESSMENT

WHAT WE ASSESS FOR RIDGE CAPPING REPAIRS PERTH.

01

Pointing condition

Cracked, missing or separated-looking pointing is identified by its position and extent along the ridge.

02

Bedding and caps

Cap alignment and the bedding relationship are considered without assuming repointing and rebedding are interchangeable.

03

Adjoining roofline

PRACTICAL ENQUIRY NOTES

PREPARE A RIDGE CAPPING REPAIR ENQUIRY.

  • Describe whether the ridge issue appears isolated or continues along the roofline.
  • Use safe ground-level photos that show the ridge and adjacent tiles.
  • Mention nearby valleys, internal marks or prior ridge work if known.

SAFE PREPARATION

RECORD, DO NOT RISK ACCESS.

Use ground-level observations and photographs where safe. Avoid climbing onto a roof, entering an unsafe ceiling space or disturbing a roof component to gather evidence.
