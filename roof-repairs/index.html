PROFESSIONAL SERVICE SCOPE

Tile and metal roof repairs — the work we carry out

Ellis Services Group repairs damaged roof coverings and the connections that keep rain outside. Our Perth team replaces broken tiles, repairs metal fixings and sheets, restores ridge caps and renews affected valleys and flashings. We inspect adjoining components so the work addresses the roof system around the defect.

The repair scope identifies the components, materials and access needed for your property. Local repairs address isolated damage where surrounding roofing remains serviceable; wider component work is arranged when inspection identifies more extensive deterioration. Leak investigation and drainage repairs are coordinated with the covering work where they are connected.

Small roof repairs without an unnecessary wider scope.

Can I arrange a local repair rather than a full restoration?

Yes. A defined tile, metal, ridge or flashing defect can be assessed for a local repair. We check the surrounding material before setting the scope; a small visible defect can connect with other damage. Cleaning or painting is a separate decision, not a condition of every repair.

For a rental property, include the tenant or access contact and who can approve the work. Ask for the repair items, access, materials, removal of waste and exclusions to be clear in the quote. Any warranty terms should be confirmed in writing for that scope, rather than assumed from a general website statement.

SERVICE QUESTIONS

Questions about roof repairs perth.

Which roof repair service suits a leak, damaged tile or metal roof detail?

Choose leak repairs for water entry, tile repairs for damaged concrete or terracotta tiles, or metal-roof repairs for sheet and fixing faults. If you are unsure, Ellis inspects the roof on site and identifies the work required.

What should a roof repair quote identify before work is discussed?

A written roof repair quote should name the affected area, findings, components to repair, materials, access and included work. Ellis also explains exclusions and any further investigation before you approve the work.

When is targeted roof repair more suitable than a broader roof restoration?

A local repair suits an isolated defect when surrounding roof materials remain sound. Widespread deterioration calls for a broader assessment; Ellis compares repair and restoration options and quotes for the recommended scope.
