PRACTICAL ENQUIRY NOTES

PREPARE A CLEAR ROOF REPAIR ENQUIRY.

  • Name the roof area or room where the change is visible.
  • Share the roof material where known and safe photographs from ground level.
  • Note when the issue first appeared and whether weather or a previous repair is relevant.

SAFE PREPARATION

RECORD, DO NOT RISK ACCESS.

Use ground-level observations and photographs where safe. Avoid climbing onto a roof, entering an unsafe ceiling space or disturbing a roof component to gather evidence.
