PROFESSIONAL SERVICE SCOPE

Tile and metal roof repairs — the work we carry out

Ellis Services Group repairs damaged roof coverings and the connections that keep rain outside. Our Perth team replaces broken tiles, repairs metal fixings and sheets, restores ridge caps and renews affected valleys and flashings. We inspect adjoining components so the work addresses the roof system around the defect.

The repair scope identifies the components, materials and access needed for your property. Local repairs address isolated damage where surrounding roofing remains serviceable; wider component work is arranged when inspection identifies more extensive deterioration. Leak investigation and drainage repairs are coordinated with the covering work where they are connected.

Roof repair cost factors in Perth

The cost of a roof repair depends on the defect, the surrounding material and the access needed to do the work. A leaking joint, a damaged tile and a corroded metal connection are different jobs. We assess the affected area before providing a written quote; a suburb or ceiling photograph alone is not enough to price the repair.

Material, extent and connected components

For tiles, the replacement profile and the condition of nearby tiles or ridge details matter. For metal roofing, a fixing repair may also involve the sheet around the fixing or an adjoining flashing. Ask which components are included, what can remain in place and whether any adjacent defects need a separate item.

Access and preparation

Building height, roof pitch, restricted access and structures below the work area can change the equipment and preparation required. Include these details when requesting an assessment. Safe access is part of planning the job, not an optional extra to leave out when comparing quotes.

Repair quote inclusions beyond the total price

A useful comparison identifies the repairs, materials, access arrangements, preparation, removal of affected material and completion checks included. Confirm any inspection charge, exclusions and how additional work would be agreed if opening the area reveals further deterioration. Ask for optional surface work to be separated from repairs needed to address the defect.
