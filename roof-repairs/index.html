Roof repairs

Roof repairs

We repair tile and metal roofs in Perth, including leaks, damaged tiles, capping and flashings. We inspect the roof before providing a written repair quote.

Local repairs for damaged roof areas

A damaged tile, sheet, flashing or gutter connection may only need a local repair if the surrounding roof is still in good condition. When several areas need attention, we can plan the repairs together. Cleaning and painting are separate jobs.

Roof inspection, written quote and repairs

  1. Inspect the damaged area and nearby roof and drainage connections, taking previous repairs into account.
  2. Provide a written quote listing what will be repaired or replaced, the materials, roof access, preparation and rubbish removal.
  3. Complete the agreed repairs and check the repaired joints and drainage. Explain any work that is not included or needs further investigation before it goes ahead.

Any warranty terms must be confirmed in writing for the particular scope.

Choosing repairs and comparing quotes

Repairs for leaks, tiles and metal roofs

Water coming indoors may need leak investigation. Broken concrete or terracotta tiles need tile repairs, while damaged sheets or fixings need metal roof repairs. If you are unsure, tell us what you have noticed and we will inspect the roof.

Items included in a roof repair quote

Our written roof repair quote lists the affected area, what we found, the repairs, materials and access arrangements. We explain exclusions and any further investigation before you approve the work.

Local repairs or more extensive work

An isolated fault may suit a local repair when nearby roofing is sound. If deterioration is widespread, we inspect the wider area and explain the repair and restoration options before quoting.
