Roof repairs

Roof repairs

We inspect and repair Perth tile and metal roofs, tracing leaks and restoring damaged tiles, ridges, valleys, flashings and roof-edge drainage. Our team defines the work required and coordinates the repairs for your property.

Request a roof assessment

Roof defects and connected components

We repair damaged tiles, metal sheets, ridges, valleys, flashings and roof-edge drainage. An isolated defect can suit a local repair where surrounding material remains serviceable; several connected defects can be coordinated in one scope. Cleaning or painting is a separate surface decision.

Leak, tile and metal repair options

  1. Inspect the affected roof area and adjoining water path, including earlier repairs and concealed areas accessible within the agreed scope.
  2. Specify the components to retain, repair or replace, compatible materials, preparation, access and waste removal in a written quote.
  3. Complete the agreed repairs and review the repaired connections and relevant drainage details. Explain any exclusions or further work before it proceeds.

For a rental, identify who can approve the work and arrange access. Any warranty terms must be confirmed in writing for the particular scope. A universal online price cannot describe every roof.

Repair findings, materials and access

Leak, tile and metal repair scopes

Choose leak repairs for water entry, tile repairs for damaged concrete or terracotta tiles, or metal-roof repairs for sheet and fixing faults. If you are unsure, Ellis inspects the roof on site and identifies the work required.

Roof repair quote findings, materials and access

A written roof repair quote should name the affected area, findings, components to repair, materials, access and included work. Ellis also explains exclusions and any further investigation before you approve the work.

Targeted repairs for isolated roof defects

A local repair suits an isolated defect when surrounding roof materials remain sound. Widespread deterioration calls for a broader assessment; Ellis compares repair and restoration options and quotes for the recommended scope.
